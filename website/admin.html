How verification works

  1. A citizen raises an SOS in your city - it waits as Waiting for verification, and you get an e-mail.
  2. Call the reporter on the phone number shown in the Requests tab and check the details.
  3. Verify a genuine SOS - relief can then be dispatched with Smart Match - or reject a fake one with a reason. Every decision is in the audit log.

How it works for your organization

  1. Offer supplies in the Resources tab - add what you hold (food, water, medicine, shelter, vehicles) and restock it any time.
  2. See what is needed in the Requests tab - live SOS requests, sorted by priority.
  3. The CrisisConnect team decides dispatch. Every offer alerts the administrator, who runs Smart Match; your stock is then sent to the most urgent nearby requests.
